# Redirect.txt admin

The settings keep the established WordPress typography, spacing, and controls. Keep the
editor and its save action as the focus of the Rules screen.

Place rule-level feedback on the affected line. Use a low-contrast fill across the editor's
inner width and keep the text, caret, and line height aligned. Show one short explanation
in the free space beside the active rule. If space is tight, place it at the edge of the
editor where it leaves every rule visible. Provide the same explanation to mouse, keyboard,
and touch users.
